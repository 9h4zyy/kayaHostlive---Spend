What Is Kaya Chat & What Does a Host Do?

Kaya Chat is a live voice party app. You can drop into voice rooms, meet people, send virtual gifts, earn coins, and climb the leaderboards. Anyone can join. Becoming a host is where it gets interesting.

A Kaya Chat host is a user who takes part in live voice rooms and can host conversations of their own. Hosts welcome people into the room, chat, sing, and interact with participants.

How to Become a Kaya Chat Host, Step by Step

  1. 1
    Download Kaya Chat from Google Play or the App Store, or open the referral link.
  2. 2
    Sign up with a Google account or your phone number. Google sign-up is recommended.
  3. 3
    Fill in the required profile information.
  4. 4
    Open your Profile page and tap “Become a Host.”
  5. 5
    Select “Real Person Verification.” Upload your own photo, tap “Start Verification,” and follow the on-screen steps. Keep your face clearly visible, with no mask, sunglasses, or camera filters.
  6. 6
    Select “Join Agency,” enter the agency invitation code CVK6M5, then tap “Confirm.”

Kaya Chat Host Earning Sources

1
First 7 days. New hosts can get 8,000 diamonds for one hour on the mic per day, for up to 7 days. Claim it in the Task Center each day, or it expires.
2
Day Income Task. Meet the listed 7-day diamond target and the required mic hours to qualify for higher hourly rewards. Run from 600 diamonds per hour up to 250,000 at level SS.
3
Gift sharing. Deluxe gifts carry a 70% share. Lucky gifts carry 2% and 5%.
4
Host invitation bonus. Earn diamonds when hosts you invite reach income milestones within 30 days up to 80,000 diamonds.
5
Task and event rankings. Categories include the Weekly Agency Ranking and Hosts Ranking.
